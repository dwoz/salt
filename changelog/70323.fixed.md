Fix two async-task lifecycle bugs in ``Minion.process_state_queue`` /
``process_process_queue`` and the ``io_loop.close()`` shutdown path in
``salt/minion.py``.  The periodic-callback guard flags
(``_state_queue_processing_active`` / ``_process_queue_processing_active``)
now always reset in the outer async wrapper's ``finally`` so an unhandled
exception inside the impl no longer wedges the queue drain for the
remainder of the minion's lifetime.  Pending Tasks on ``self.io_loop`` are
also cancelled and drained via ``asyncio.gather`` before ``io_loop.close()``
at three shutdown sites, eliminating ``Task was destroyed but it is
pending!`` and ``coroutine '...' was never awaited`` warnings on minion,
multi-minion, and syndic shutdown.
