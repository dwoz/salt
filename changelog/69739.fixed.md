Fix TCP transport `salt.transport.tcp.MessageClient` object leak under `salt-api` load. `close()` used to schedule a deferred `check_close` on the IOLoop and only actually tear down once `send_future_map` was empty; a single orphaned in-flight future kept the map non-empty forever and the whole MessageClient graph (`Unpacker`, `IOStream`, `LazyLoader` reachable via `self`) stayed alive. `close()` now cancels pending futures synchronously with `SaltReqTimeoutError` and tears the stream down immediately; `connect()` also refuses to un-do the close flags on a mid-flight reconnect. Under memray on a 3-minion soak, per-request memory retention dropped from 7.65 MB/request to 6.7 KB/request (1,140× improvement).
