Fixed a per-subscriber leak in ``salt.transport.tcp.PubServer`` where the ``_stream_read`` coroutine's 1 MiB ``msgpack.Unpacker`` buffer and the ``client -> _read_task -> coroutine frame -> client`` reference cycle survived stream close until the next cyclic-GC pass, and where the close-callback path leaked the per-subscriber ``self._writers`` ``(asyncio.Queue, drain-Task)`` tuple. ``_stream_read`` now releases the Unpacker and clears ``client._read_task`` in a ``try/finally`` so refcount collection reclaims the coroutine frame immediately, and ``_discard_on_close._cb()`` also pops the ``_writers`` entry and cancels the drain task, matching what ``_discard_slow_client`` already does. See issue #70175.
